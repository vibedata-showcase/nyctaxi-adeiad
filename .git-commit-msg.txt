Add billable measures and reporting region to the daily zone aggregate

agg_trip_zone_daily now publishes the billable population beside its all-trip
columns, so the finance daily zone revenue report can read one relation instead
of aggregating fct_trip a second time (ADR 0018). The existing columns keep their
names, positions, types and values; the new ones are appended.

Each billable sum is coalesced to zero: a zone-day whose trips are all
non-billable has no filtered row to add up, and the design says such a row
carries zero rather than null. That is recorded as D-07, superseding D-06.

Also adds reporting_region, which the report publishes and the table did not
carry; re-joining dim_zone downstream would fan out across seven SCD2 zone keys.
