# Verification: Borough-to-borough trips and revenue

## Discovery

Recorded during design. Each result is the query exactly as it ran against `domain`.

- `core.fct_trip` spans the whole reporting window and carries no null zone key: 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31, 97,184 trips, 95,958 billable, 0 null pickup keys, 0 null dropoff keys. All 91 days carry at least one trip, so no month is cut short. — `select min(pickup_datetime)::date as min_dt, max(pickup_datetime)::date as max_dt, count(*) as trips, count(*) filter (where is_billable) as billable, count(*) filter (where pickup_zone_key is null) as null_pickup_key, count(*) filter (where dropoff_zone_key is null) as null_dropoff_key from core.fct_trip`
- The borough domain is eight values, two of them unresolved and two of them not NYC: Manhattan 85,245 billable pickups, Queens 8,672, Brooklyn 1,302, Bronx 342, Unknown 314, N/A 46, Staten Island 25, EWR 12. — `select z.borough_name, z.is_unresolved_zone, count(*) as trips, min(z.borough_code) as code from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone z on f.pickup_zone_key = z.zone_key where f.is_billable group by 1,2 order by 3 desc`
- Over all eight borough values a month has 40–42 occupied origin-destination pairs, 21–23 of them under 25 trips, and the thin cells are exactly the Staten Island and Bronx–outer-borough pairs. — `with m as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date as mth, o.borough_name as ob, d.borough_name as db, count(*) as trips, sum(f.total_amount) as rev from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key = o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key = d.zone_key where f.is_billable and f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' group by 1,2,3) select mth, count(*) as borough_pairs, sum(case when trips < 25 then 1 else 0 end) pairs_under_25, sum(trips) as trips, min(trips) as min_trips from m group by 1 order by 1`
- Restricted to trips whose pickup and dropoff zones are both in an NYC borough, the months hold 22, 20 and 23 occupied pairs of the 25 cells, with 29,568 / 29,650 / 35,505 trips and 785,518.24 / 785,082.37 / 963,284.41 revenue. Q1 totals: 94,723 trips, 2,533,885.02 revenue. — `with b as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date mth, o.borough_name ob, d.borough_name db, count(*) c, sum(f.total_amount) rev from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key=o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key=d.zone_key where f.is_billable and o.is_nyc and d.is_nyc and f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' group by 1,2,3) select mth, sum(c) as trips, round(sum(rev),2) as revenue, count(*) as pairs_present from b group by 1 order by 1`
- The three empty cells are Staten Island→Bronx in all three months; every other unoccupied cell of the NYC grid is a Staten Island outbound or inbound pair. Across the 75 cells, 36 hold fewer than 25 trips. — `with b as (select unnest(['Manhattan','Bronx','Brooklyn','Queens','Staten Island']) as b), months as (select unnest([date '2024-01-01', date '2024-02-01', date '2024-03-01']) as mth), spine as (select months.mth, b1.b as ob, b2.b as db from months, b b1, b b2), obs as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date as mth, o.borough_name ob, d.borough_name db, count(*) c from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key=o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key=d.zone_key where f.is_billable and o.is_nyc and d.is_nyc and f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' group by 1,2,3) select s.mth, s.ob, s.db, coalesce(o.c,0) as trips from spine s left join obs o using (mth, ob, db) where coalesce(o.c,0) < 25 order by 1,4 desc`
- The origin share ranges from 5.21% (Manhattan, February) to 73.13% (Queens, March) and rests on as few as 2 trips (Staten Island, February, share 0.00%): January Manhattan 5.30% over 26,394 trips, Queens 70.52% over 2,710, Brooklyn 37.18% over 347, Bronx 58.88% over 107, Staten Island 40.00% over 10. — `with b as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date mth, o.borough_name ob, d.borough_name db, count(*) c from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key=o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key=d.zone_key where f.is_billable and o.is_nyc and d.is_nyc and f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' group by 1,2,3) select mth, ob, sum(c) as origin_trips, sum(case when ob<>db then c else 0 end) as cross_trips, round(100.0*sum(case when ob<>db then c else 0 end)/sum(c),2) as cross_share_pct from b group by 1,2 order by 1, origin_trips desc`
- The cross-borough share moves with the population: 12.73% over every billable trip, 12.08% over trips whose two zones both resolve, 11.88% over trips whose two zones are both NYC boroughs. 1,000 Q1 billable trips touch an unresolved zone and 1,235 touch a non-NYC one. — `with b as (select o.borough_name ob, d.borough_name db, f.total_amount, o.is_unresolved_zone ou, d.is_unresolved_zone du, o.is_nyc ony, d.is_nyc dny, f.pickup_datetime from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key=o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key=d.zone_key where f.is_billable and f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01') select count(*) as billable_trips, round(100.0*count(*) filter (where ob<>db)/count(*),2) as cross_pct_all, round(100.0*count(*) filter (where ob<>db and not ou and not du and ony and dny)/count(*) filter (where not ou and not du and ony and dny),2) as cross_pct_nyc_only, round(100.0*count(*) filter (where ob<>db and not ou and not du)/count(*) filter (where not ou and not du),2) as cross_pct_resolved, count(*) filter (where ou or du) as trips_touching_unresolved, count(*) filter (where not ony or not dny) as trips_touching_non_nyc, round(sum(total_amount),2) as revenue from b`
- Billable revenue and payment-method-recognised revenue differ by about 1.2% a month, which is why the choice was asked: January 814,780.86 against 805,326.52, February 812,329.21 against 802,407.58, March 998,524.68 against 986,590.20. — `select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date as mth, count(*) as billable_trips, round(sum(f.total_amount),2) as billable_revenue, count(*) filter (where p.counts_as_revenue) as recognised_trips, round(sum(case when p.counts_as_revenue then f.total_amount else 0 end),2) as recognised_revenue from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_payment_method p on f.payment_method_key = p.payment_method_key where f.is_billable and f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' group by 1 order by 1`
- Every trip's zone surrogate is the version in force on its own pickup date, so the two zone joins do not multiply rows: 0 trips fall outside their zone's half-open validity interval. — `select count(*) as trips_with_overlapping_zone_versions from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone z on f.pickup_zone_key=z.zone_key where not (f.pickup_datetime >= z.valid_from_date and f.pickup_datetime < z.valid_to_date + 1)`
- The canonical star already answers month × origin borough × destination borough through its detail source, and no aggregate source can: the compiler chose `LTS1_detail` and rejected all seven aggregate sources, the zone-bearing ones because rolling a versioned zone up from an aggregate needs the fact's date. January's Bronx→Bronx cell reads 44 billable trips and 1,085.21 revenue both from the star and from the independent query above. — `semantic_query` on `nyc_taxi_trips`, metrics `billable_trip_count`, `billable_revenue`, group by `trips_to_date`/`month`, `trips_to_pickup_zone`/`borough`, `trips_to_dropoff_zone`/`borough`.
