- Billable trips are 95,958 of the 97,184, all inside the window, and none of them lacks a zone key or a date key: 0 null pickup zone keys, 0 null dropoff zone keys, 0 null date keys — `select count(*) as fct_trip_rows, count(*) filter (where pickup_date >= date '2024-01-01' and pickup_date < date '2024-04-01') as in_window, count(*) filter (where is_billable) as billable_all, count(*) filter (where is_billable and pickup_zone_key is null) as billable_null_pickup_zone, count(*) filter (where is_billable and dropoff_zone_key is null) as billable_null_dropoff_zone, count(distinct pickup_date) as distinct_pickup_dates from core.fct_trip` and `select count(*) filter (where is_billable and pickup_date_key is null) as billable_null_date_key, (select count(*) from core.dim_date where calendar_date >= date '2024-01-01' and calendar_date < date '2024-04-01') as dim_date_days from core.fct_trip`
- `core.dim_zone` carries 272 rows over 265 zones across 8 borough values, no null borough, versioned as documented — `select count(*) as rows, count(*) filter (where is_current_version) as current_rows, count(distinct zone_key) as distinct_zone_key, count(*) filter (where borough_name is null) as null_borough, count(distinct borough_name) as borough_values from core.dim_zone`
- The 8 borough values are Bronx, Brooklyn, EWR, Manhattan, N/A, Queens, Staten Island and Unknown; `Unknown` and `N/A` are the unresolved groups and `EWR` alone carries `is_nyc = false` without being unresolved — `select borough_name, is_nyc, is_core_market, is_unresolved_zone, count(*) as zones from core.dim_zone where is_current_version group by 1,2,3,4 order by 1`
- At month × origin borough × destination borough the Q1 2024 data yields 122 populated cells (40 / 40 / 42), summing to 95,958 trips and 2,629,185.82 revenue — `with pairs as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_date) as m, o.borough_name as ob, d.borough_name as db, count(*) as trips, sum(f.total_amount) as rev from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key = o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key = d.zone_key where f.is_billable and f.pickup_date >= date '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_date < date '2024-04-01' group by 1,2,3) select m, round(sum(rev),2) as revenue, count(*) as cells, count(distinct ob) as origins, count(distinct db) as dests from pairs group by 1 order by 1`
- 66 of those 122 cells carry fewer than 25 trips, and they hold 253 of the 95,958 trips and 19,966.06 of the 2,629,185.82 revenue — `with pairs as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_date) as m, o.borough_name as ob, d.borough_name as db, count(*) as trips, sum(f.total_amount) as rev from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key = o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key = d.zone_key where f.is_billable group by 1,2,3) select count(*) as pair_months, min(trips) as min_trips, count(*) filter (where trips < 25) as under_25, sum(trips) as total_trips from pairs` and `with pairs as (the same subquery) select sum(trips) filter (where trips >= 25) as trips_kept_25, sum(trips) filter (where trips < 25) as trips_dropped_25, sum(rev) filter (where trips >= 25) as rev_kept_25, sum(rev) as rev_all from pairs`

- EWR traffic in Q1 2024 appears on both axes: 12 billable trips departed it (6 / 2 / 4 by month), and 235 billable trips arrived in it — `select d.borough_name as dest_borough, count(*) as trips from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key = d.zone_key where f.is_billable and d.borough_name = 'EWR' group by 1`, with the monthly origin row from the origin-borough query below
- The crossing share over the whole departing population reads 12.79% (3,834 of 29,985), 12.15% (3,647 of 30,017) and 13.16% (4,733 of 35,956); restricted to pairs where both ends resolve it would be 3,581, 3,425 and 4,466 of the same denominators — `with p as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_date) as m, o.borough_name as ob, d.borough_name as db, count(*) as trips from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key = o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key = d.zone_key where f.is_billable group by 1, 2, 3) select m, sum(trips) as all_trips, sum(trips) filter (where ob <> db) as cross_all, sum(trips) filter (where ob <> db and ob not in ('Unknown','N/A') and db not in ('Unknown','N/A')) as cross_resolved, sum(trips) filter (where ob in ('Unknown','N/A') or db in ('Unknown','N/A')) as touches_unresolved from p group by 1 order by 1`
