## Findings

- **`resolved_trip_count` / `cross_borough_trip_count` are zero on unresolved origins, and the null share's stated reason is contradicted by the data.** `origin_month` in `transformation/models/marts/agg/agg_borough_flow_monthly.sql` groups over `and not o.is_unresolved_zone`, so an unresolved origin's counts never reach the row. The relation then publishes `0`/`0` beside cells that hold `484`, `2987` (January), `238`, `2834` (February) and `702`, `3784` (March) resolved-destination trips. Design `D-05` justifies the null share with "an origin with no resolved-destination trip — every unresolved origin", and ADR 0019 with "the count of its resolved-destination trips is published beside it as the basis"; both are false of this data, and `_agg.yml`, the contract and the generated docs repeat the second. No test covers the case: `assert_borough_flow_share_matches_its_counts` filters to resolved rows and `assert_borough_flow_unresolved_cells_carry_no_share` checks the null and `trip_count` only. The column values changed by a fix are warning-free, but whether an unresolved-origin row publishes its real basis (`484`) or a deliberate `0` is a stated-reason decision the design record has to make, not one execution can pick — hence `design`, whose amendment then routes the ADR sentence through `domain-modeling` and the SQL, descriptions and a singular test through `plan`.
