## Acceptance checks

Every check below is independent of the model's own SQL: each recomputes from `core.fct_trip` or `core.dim_zone` and compares.

**R-01 — grain and pair set.** Grain uniqueness: `select count(*) as dup_grain_groups from (select month_start_date, origin_borough, dest_borough, count(*) c from agg.agg_borough_flow_monthly group by 1,2,3 having count(*) > 1)` → `dup_grain_groups = 0`. Row counts per month, from the built relation: 40 / 40 / 42 for 2024-01 / 2024-02 / 2024-03, matching the independent pair count from `core.fct_trip` (`borough_pairs` 40 / 40 / 42) taken before authoring. **Pass.**

**R-02 — trip population closure.** Independent recomputation of every pair's count and revenue from `core.fct_trip` joined to `core.dim_date` and `core.dim_zone` twice over the same window and billable filter, full-outer-joined to the built relation on (month, origin, destination), counting rows where `trip_count` differs or the revenue differs by more than half a cent: **`mismatched_rows = 0`**. Per-month totals from the built relation are 29,985 / 30,017 / 35,956, equal to the star's billable counts for those months. **Pass.**

**R-03 — revenue and its coverage.** Same recomputation as R-02, at the cent: 0 mismatches. The built per-month revenue is 816,057.76 / 813,885.68 / 999,242.38, matching the star's `sum(total_amount)` over billable trips. `trips_with_revenue` equals `trip_count` on every row, as expected since no billable trip in Q1 has a null total. **Pass.**

**R-04 — the share and its parts.** Independent recomputation of every origin-month's share from `core.fct_trip` — cross-borough trips over resolved-destination trips, null for an unresolved origin — full-outer-joined to the built relation: **`mismatched_origins = 0`**. Series consistency: `count(distinct origin_trip_count)`, `count(distinct origin_resolved_trip_count)`, `count(distinct cross_borough_trip_count)` and `count(distinct cross_borough_share)` per (month, origin) are all 1, so `rows_with_repeated_totals_disagreeing = 0`. `assert_borough_flow_denominator_adds_up` holds `resolved + unresolved = total` on every row. Spot value: Manhattan's January share is 5.55%, the figure computed from `core.fct_trip` before the model existed. **Pass.**

**R-05 — contract and documentation.** The relation is a boundary model in `agg` with `contract: {enforced: true}`; the build passes; `odcs.py check`, `check --relations` and `profile` all pass with no finding for it; every column carries a description. **Pass.**

## Docs match data

- The model description says one row per month and borough pair carrying at least one billable trip: the built relation has 40 / 40 / 42 rows against 40 / 40 / 42 observed pairs, no zero rows.
- "No 25-trip floor applies here": the built relation publishes pairs well under 25 trips (e.g. Staten Island → Manhattan, 1 trip in January), and `sum(trip_count)` reconciles to the star — so the population is the whole one, not a floored subset.
- "Trips ending in an unresolved borough keep their row but leave the share's denominator": `origin_unresolved_trip_count` is non-zero where such trips exist (128 for Manhattan in January) and `origin_resolved_trip_count` excludes exactly those.
- The share values quoted in ADR 0018 (12.79% / 12.15% / 13.16% with every destination counted, 12.57% / 12.05% / 12.99% if pairs under 25 trips were withheld) are a comparison of the floor's effect on one denominator, computed from `core.fct_trip` before authoring. They are not the published figure: the relation's denominator excludes unresolved destinations (R-04), so the all-borough published share is **11.94% / 11.47% / 12.48%**, and Manhattan's is **5.55% / 5.42% / 5.60%**. Every figure in this paragraph is a separate measurement and none is interchangeable with another.
- ADR 0018's "40 to 42 pairs a month" and "99 / 55 / 99 trips withheld" are the figures observed in `core.fct_trip` for Q1 2024.
