# Requirement: Borough-to-borough flow, Q1 2024

## Ask

Leila Haddad, Mobility Planning Lead: "The city asked how much of our traffic crosses borough lines. For each month of Q1 2024, I need the trips and the revenue from each borough to each borough, and the share of trips that leave the borough they started in."

A new boundary table the city and Mobility Planning read directly: three months of borough-to-borough trip and revenue cells, with each origin borough's share of trips that end in another borough.

## Requirements

- [ ] `R-01` One row per (month, origin borough, destination borough) for each month of Q1 2024 — accept when the relation has exactly 3 × 8 × 8 = 192 rows, the three months being 2024-01-01, 2024-02-01 and 2024-03-01 and the eight borough values the TLC zone lookup holds — source: request ("for each month of Q1 2024 … from each borough to each borough"); answer to Q1
- [ ] `R-02` Every borough pair appears in every month, including the 12 pair-months where no trip ran, which carry a trip count of zero and a revenue of zero — accept when no (month, origin borough, destination borough) combination in `dim_zone` is absent from the relation — source: answer to Q1
- [ ] `R-03` Each row carries the number of trips and the revenue for that pair; a trip is in scope when its total is positive (billable) and its pickup lies in the reporting window — accept when a row's trip count and revenue equal a recomputation from `fct_trip` filtered on `is_billable` and the reporting window — source: request; ADR 0003 ("A new revenue or flow mart applies the rule"); ADR 0003 ("inside the reporting window")
- [ ] `R-04` Each row carries its origin borough's share of trips that leave that borough, as a percentage of the borough's trips whose destination is a resolved borough; the share reads null on an origin whose borough is unresolved (N/A, Unknown) — accept when the share equals the month's cross-borough trips over the month's resolved-destination trips for that origin, and is null for both unresolved origins — source: request ("the share of trips that leave the borough they started in"); answers to Q3 and Q4
- [ ] `R-05` A trip to or from an unresolved zone (borough N/A or Unknown) is neither in-borough nor cross-borough: it stays in the table in its own group but never enters a share — accept when the relation's rows for destinations N/A and Unknown carry a null share on every origin, and the share's denominator excludes those trips — source: answer to Q3; ADR 0012
- [ ] `R-06` No minimum-volume floor applies: a borough pair is published whatever its volume — accept when the relation holds pairs below 25 trips for a month and `sum(trip_count)` over the relation equals the billable trips of the window — source: answer to Q2; ADR 0018
- [ ] `R-07` Revenue is the sum of `total_amount` over the pair's billable trips, and a null total does not make the cell null — accept when a recomputation of the sum matches the published value on every row — source: answer to Q5
- [ ] `R-08` The relation reconciles to the star on both measures: the sum of `trip_count` over all rows equals the billable trips of `fct_trip` in the reporting window, and the sum of `total_revenue` over all rows equals the billable revenue of that window — accept when both aggregates are equal to their `fct_trip` counterparts, 9,604,511 trips and 263,480,834.99 of revenue at the time of writing — source: ADR 0003; repository `fct_trip`; user answer ("the sum of revenue over all rows must equal billable revenue for the reporting window (263,480,834.99 for Q1 2024)")
- [ ] `R-09` The table is a new boundary relation, published whole with its documentation: a dbt model with its properties entry, a design record, a data contract, its place in the semantic layer and the ontology, and the tests that prove R-01 to R-08 — accept when the model builds in the sandbox, its tests pass, and its contract and semantic-layer entry agree with the built relation — source: repository `repo-map.json` (`layers`, `contracts`, `logical_model`); product change

## Business decisions

| Decision | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grain of the borough flow table | One row per month × origin borough × destination borough, the month being `month_start_date` | Request ("for each month … from each borough to each borough") |
| Edge periods | No period is cut short: the data window covers 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31 at 31, 29 and 31 days, so all three months are whole | Repository `dim_date`, `fct_trip` (queried) |
| Which pairs are published | The full 8 × 8 borough grid for each month; a pair-month with no trip is published as a row of zero | Answer to Q1 |
| Minimum-volume floor | None: borough pairs are published at any volume, unlike the zone-pair matrix under ADR 0017 | Answer to Q2; ADR 0018 |
| Trip population | Billable trips inside the reporting window, per ADR 0003 | ADR 0003 ("A new revenue or flow mart applies the rule") |
| Cross-borough definition | A trip counts as leaving its borough when its pickup borough and its dropoff borough are both resolved and differ; EWR is a borough as the TLC lists it; N/A and Unknown are not boroughs | Answer to Q3; ADR 0019 |
| Share on an unresolved origin | Null, with the resolved-destination trip count published beside it as the basis | Answer to Q4 |
| Null revenue | `sum()` ignores a null `total_amount`; a null total never makes a cell null (no billable trip in Q1 2024 carries one) | Answer to Q5 |
| Coverage of the revenue measure | `trip_count` is published on every row beside the revenue, so the reader sees how many trips a cell's revenue speaks for | Playbook default (`transformation`, Coverage) |
| Reuse | Not built on `agg_od_flow_matrix`: that model's ADR 0017 floor and zone grain exclude exactly the pairs and the denominator this table must carry, so it is built from `fct_trip` and `dim_zone` | ADR 0017; answer to Q2; playbook (`transformation`, Reuse) |
| Unmatched and null keys | Every in-window billable trip resolves to a `dim_zone` row (0 null pickup and 0 null dropoff zone keys in Q1 2024); the unresolved zones are real dimension rows, retained in their own group per ADR 0012 | ADR 0012; ADR 0015; `fct_trip` (queried) |
| Extended scope | `not applicable`: no schedule, and the semantic-layer, ontology and contract deliverables stay inside `transformation` | Playbook (`transformation`, Extended scope) |

## Out of scope

- `agg_od_flow_matrix` and its 25-trip floor (ADR 0017): unchanged by this intent.
- Zone-grain flow, day or daypart grain, and any period outside Q1 2024.
- The reporting window variables in `dbt_project.yml` (ADR 0003): unchanged.
- Fleet-operations and quality marts.
