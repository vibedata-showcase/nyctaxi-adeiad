# Requirement: mich

## Ask

The Mobility Planning Lead, Leila Haddad, needs the borough-to-borough traffic matrix for the city: for each month of Q1 2024, the trips and the revenue from each borough to each borough, and the share of trips that leave the borough they started in.

The consumer is Mobility Planning, answering a question the city asked about how much traffic crosses borough lines. The result is a new gold relation in the Domain's `agg` layer; nothing published today reports a destination borough.

## Requirements

- [ ] `R-01` One row per calendar month of Q1 2024, origin borough and destination borough, carrying the billable trips and the billable revenue of that pair in that month — accept when the relation holds 122 rows (Jan 40, Feb 40, Mar 42, the pair-months that carry traffic), no duplicate on (month, origin borough, destination borough), and `sum(trip_count)` = 95,958 and `sum(revenue)` = 2,629,185.82, matching `core.fct_trip` over the reporting window — source: request; `core.fct_trip`, `core.dim_zone`; counts from the Q1 2024 data
- [ ] `R-02` Both measures cover billable trips inside the reporting window only, and revenue is `sum(total_amount)` — accept when each month's revenue equals that month's total in `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` (Jan 816,057.76, Feb 813,885.68, Mar 999,242.38) and each month's trip count equals that mart's trip total (29,985 / 30,017 / 35,956) — source: ADR 0003, which puts a new revenue or flow mart under the rule; `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue`
- [ ] `R-03` Each row carries the share of that origin borough's month trips that ended in a different borough, over the trips that departed that borough in that month — accept when the crossing count of each origin-month equals the sum of its rows whose destination differs from the origin, and when Manhattan reads 6.01% in January (1,598 of 26,593), 5.78% in February (1,554 of 26,908) and 5.97% in March (1,895 of 31,744) — source: user answer on the denominator; recomputed from the Q1 2024 data
- [ ] `R-04` The borough axis is every borough value `dim_zone` carries, unresolved groups (`Unknown`, `N/A`) and the non-NYC `EWR` included, so a trip ending in an unresolved borough counts as having crossed a borough line — accept when all 8 borough values appear as origins and as destinations, and the Manhattan → `Unknown` cell is present (88 / 74 / 85 trips in January, February, March) — source: ADR 0018, from the user's answers
- [ ] `R-05` Every borough pair that carries billable traffic is published, with no 25-trip floor — accept when the relation includes the 66 pair-months under 25 trips and its totals are exactly the window's billable totals with no shortfall — source: ADR 0019, from the user's answer; ADR 0017 stays as written for the zone-pair matrix
- [ ] `R-06` A borough pair with no billable trip in a month has no row — accept when no row of the relation carries `trip_count` = 0 — source: user answer
- [ ] `R-07` A billable trip whose pickup or dropoff resolves to no zone version in force is left out of the matrix, and how many were left out is published so the gap can be seen — accept when what ships carries, per month, the count of billable in-window trips with a null pickup or dropoff zone key, and each Q1 2024 month reads 0 — source: user answer; ADR 0015, ADR 0018
- [ ] `R-08` The new relation is a gold surface like its peers: it carries an enforced dbt contract, an ODCS contract under `contracts/`, a binding in the trips canonical document, and a design record — accept when `validating-data-contract` and `validating-semantic-model` pass against it and the design record cites ADR 0018 and ADR 0019 — source: `repo-map.json` (`contracts`, `logical_model`); ADR 0003; `.agents/skills/data-contract/SKILL.md`
- [ ] `R-09` Every relation published or contracted today keeps its current rows, columns and values — accept when no existing model's compiled output changes — source: request ("add a table"), ADR 0017 unchanged

## Business decisions

| Decision | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grain of the new mart | One row per calendar month × origin borough × destination borough, month being the calendar month of the trip's pickup date inside the reporting window | request; `core.dim_date` |
| Edge periods | Not applicable: every month of Q1 2024 is fully covered by the data — 31, 29 and 31 distinct pickup dates from 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31 — so no period is cut short and no row needs an incomplete mark | `core.fct_trip`, queried |
| Population | Billable trips (`fct_trip.is_billable`) with a pickup date inside the reporting window 2024-01-01 to 2024-04-01, joined to the zone version in force on the pickup date | ADR 0003; `transformation/dbt_project.yml` vars |
| Trip metric | `count(*)` over the population; a trip is counted once, in the origin borough and destination borough its zone keys resolve to | request; ADR 0015 |
| Revenue metric | `sum(total_amount)` over the population, attributed to the origin borough and the destination borough | request; `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` uses the same measure |
| Aggregation over a nullable measure | Not applicable: `is_billable` is `total_amount > 0`, so every row of the population carries a non-null total — 0 nulls in 95,958 billable trips | `int_trip_quality_flags.sql`; `core.fct_trip`, queried |
| Coverage | The measure is always present in the population, and `trip_count` beside `revenue` is the count of rows that carry it | playbook default, satisfied by the population |
| Period with no qualifying rows | Omitted: no row for a borough pair with no billable trip in the month | user answer; ADR 0019 |
| 25-trip floor (ADR 0017) | Does not apply to the borough-pair grain; every pair that carries traffic is published | user answer; ADR 0019 |
| Unmatched and null keys | A billable trip without a resolvable pickup or dropoff zone is left out of the matrix, and the number left out is published | user answer; ADR 0015 |
| Unresolved boroughs in the crossing share | A trip ending in `Unknown` or `N/A` counts as crossing; the axis is not restricted to the five NYC boroughs | user answer; ADR 0018 |
| EWR | Included as its own borough group on both axes | user answer; ADR 0018 |
| Denominator of the crossing share | The trips that departed that origin borough in that month, so the share is per origin borough and every row of a borough carries the same value | user answer |
| Reuse | No published model serves this grain: `agg_od_flow_matrix` is zone-pair grain under the ADR 0017 floor (a filtered population), and `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` has no destination. The new mart is built from `core.fct_trip` and two `core.dim_zone` roles, and no published model is rewritten | `agg_od_flow_matrix.sql`, `mart_daily_zone_revenue.sql` |
| Extended scope | Not applicable: no schedule requested; the contract and canonical-document deliverables stay in this `transformation` intent | `.agents/skills/data-contract/SKILL.md`; `repo-map.json` |
| Layer and name of the new relation | Pending design | `.agents/skills/dbt-models/SKILL.md` |

## Out of scope

- `agg.agg_od_flow_matrix` and its 25-trip floor (ADR 0017) — unchanged.
- Every other published model, including `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` and the `ops` marts — unchanged.
- Any zone-to-zone detail; this table stops at the borough pair.
- Any period outside 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31, and any change to the reporting window.
- Ingestion: no source, pipeline or schema contract changes.
- A schedule or orchestration item for the new relation.
