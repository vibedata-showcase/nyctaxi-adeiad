# Requirement: SS · borough flow

## Ask

Leila Haddad, Mobility Planning Lead:

> "The city asked how much of our traffic crosses borough lines. For each month of Q1 2024, I need the trips and the revenue from each borough to each borough, and the share of trips that leave the borough they started in."

One new boundary table: one row per month × origin borough × destination borough, carrying the billable trips, their revenue, and the share of that origin borough's traffic that ends in another borough. Consumers are Mobility Planning, who answer the city from it, and anyone reading the semantic layer's borough-level flow surface.

## Requirements

- [ ] `R-01` The project carries one new boundary table that reports monthly billable trips and revenue from each origin borough to each destination borough, for every pair that has at least one billable trip, and publishes a row for no other pair — accept when a full outer join of the built relation against an independent group-by of `fct_trip` × `dim_zone` (joined twice) on (month, origin borough, destination borough) returns no unmatched row on either side and no trip-count or revenue difference — source: request
- [ ] `R-02` The grain is one row per (month, origin borough, destination borough) — accept when `count(*)` equals the distinct count of that triple in the built relation — source: request
- [ ] `R-03` The months are the three of Q1 2024, assigned by the trip's pickup date, with no month cut short — accept when the relation's month values are exactly 2024-01, 2024-02 and 2024-03, and each month's trip and revenue totals equal `fct_trip`'s billable totals for that month (2024-01: 29,985 trips / 816,057.76; 2024-02: 30,017 / 813,885.68; 2024-03: 35,956 / 999,242.38) — source: request; `fct_trip` Q1 2024 has all 91 days from 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31, so no period is cut short
- [ ] `R-04` The table is the whole billable population of the window, not a subset — accept when the relation's column totals are 95,958 trips and 2,629,185.82 revenue, equal to `fct_trip`'s billable in-window totals — source: user answer, "The totals must equal billable revenue, so every billable trip counts"; ADR 0003
- [ ] `R-05` No trip floor applies at this grain: a pair with one billable trip is published as it is — accept when the smallest published trip count is 1 and `R-04`'s closure holds — source: user answer, above; ADR 0018
- [ ] `R-06` Each row carries the share of that origin borough's month that ends in another borough, computed over the trips whose destination lies in a borough, with both counts it is derived from published beside it, and computed as a ratio of those two counts rather than as a mean of any row-level shares — accept when, for every origin borough and month, the published share equals an independent recomputation of (cross-borough trips ÷ located-destination trips) to four decimal places, the published numerator and denominator equal the independent counts, and re-deriving the share from the two published columns reproduces it — source: user answer, "share of that borough's own trips", base "only trips with a located destination"; ADR 0019; ADR 0016
- [ ] `R-07` A trip whose pickup or dropoff zone is unresolved, or whose zone key is null, is reported under one unresolved group in that role, carries neither an in-borough nor a cross-borough verdict, and enters neither count of `R-06`, while still counting in `R-04` — accept when an independent count finds 336 / 300 / 364 such trips in January / February / March, all present in the relation, and no share count includes them — source: user answer, "Unknown and N/A are not boroughs. Do not count their trips as in-borough or cross-borough. Keep these trips in a separate unresolved group (ADR 0012)"; ADR 0012; ADR 0015 for a null zone key
- [ ] `R-08` The borough values are the ones the master data carries — the five New York City boroughs and Newark Airport (EWR) as its own borough — with nothing remapped, split or dropped — accept when the relation's distinct origin and destination values equal `dim_zone`'s current borough values plus the unresolved group, and EWR's Q1 2024 traffic appears (235 trips ending there, 12 starting there) — source: user answer, "EWR (Newark Airport) is a borough, as the TLC lists it. Keep its trips."
- [ ] `R-09` The new relation is a boundary relation and arrives with its data contract, its semantic-layer routing and its design record — accept when its ODCS contract passes `odcs.py check`, the relation is either a star member or named in `semantic_exclusions` with its reason, and a design record at the declared path cites ADR 0018 and ADR 0019 — source: repository (`repo-map.json` `contracts`, `semantic_exclusions`; `.agents/skills/dbt-models`, `.agents/skills/data-contract`, `.agents/skills/semantic-model`)
- [ ] `R-10` The grain, the keys and the closure are guarded by tests that ship with the model and pass in the sandbox — accept when the selection's dbt data and unit tests are green and include a uniqueness test on the grain, not-null tests on the keys, a singular test reconciling the relation to `fct_trip`, and the share reproduces from its two published counts — source: repository (`.agents/skills/dbt-testing`, `test-map.json`)
- [ ] `R-11` No quality verdict removes a trip from the table: the billable trips that carry one are all still counted — accept when the relation's trip total equals the star's billable in-window total, which includes the trips `dim_trip_quality` marks zero-distance, implausibly fast or slow, sparse-feed or undocumented-rate — source: user answer at the design stop; ADR 0004

## Business decisions

| Decision | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grain of the new table | One row per (month, origin borough, destination borough), for every pair with at least one billable trip; a pair with no trips is omitted, not emitted as zero | request; user answer, "Omit pairs with no trips" |
| Edge periods | Not applicable: `fct_trip` holds all 91 days of the window (2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31), so no month is cut short and every row of a month carries the same coverage | data |
| Population: which trips | Billable only, picked up inside the reporting window — "A trip is **billable** when its total amount is positive… Gold marts that report revenue or flows apply it, inside the reporting window" | ADR 0003 |
| Population: trip floor | None. "The totals must equal billable revenue, so every billable trip counts." Departs from ADR 0017 for this grain; recorded as ADR 0018 | user answer; ADR 0018 |
| Population: the revenue measure | `sum(total_amount)` over billable trips, which includes the no-charge and dispute payment methods: "Billable revenue therefore includes them, and recognised shift revenue does not" | ADR 0013; the canonical `billable_revenue` metric |
| Population: unresolved zones | Retained in one unresolved group, counted as neither in-borough nor cross-borough, excluded from the share — "No mart excludes them on its own; a borough rollup includes an unresolved group unless its consumer filters" | ADR 0012; user answer |
| Population: a null zone key | Kept, in the unresolved group: "When a fact's lookup finds no dimension row in force, the key is null… the fact keeps one row per trip" | ADR 0015 |
| Population: the borough values | The five New York City boroughs plus EWR; the two values that place no zone are not boroughs and are reported as the unresolved group | user answer |
| Share of trips that leave the origin borough | Per origin borough, per month: cross-borough trips over the borough's trips whose destination lies in a borough. Published with both counts | user answer |
| Share where the base is empty | Not applicable in this window: every origin borough has located-destination trips, so no share is undefined | data |
| Coverage | The counts the share is derived from are published beside it, rather than a count of rows carrying a measure: the measure is derived, and its base is what a reader needs | playbook default (Coverage) |
| Quality verdicts | No verdict filters a trip: the table keeps every billable trip, and a consumer excluding verdicts reads `fct_trip` through `trip_quality_key` | ADR 0004; user answer at the design stop |
| How the share is computed | A ratio of summed counts through `safe_divide`, null on a zero denominator, with both counts published so no consumer averages the row-level shares | ADR 0016; user answer at the design stop |
| Reuse | Built from `fct_trip` with `dim_zone` joined twice, as `agg_od_flow_matrix` is; not built on that matrix, which has no month grain and carries the 25-trip floor | repository: `agg_od_flow_matrix.sql`, ADR 0017, its design record D-1 and D-3 |
| Unmatched and null keys | No inner join drops or merges a trip; a null key lands in the unresolved group | ADR 0015 |
| Extended scope | No schedule was asked for, so no `orchestration` kind: `kinds: [transformation]` | playbook default |

## Out of scope

- Any change to `fct_trip`, `dim_zone`, `agg_od_flow_matrix` or any other published model; ADR 0017's floor keeps applying to `agg_od_flow_matrix`.
- A zone-pair, service-type, daypart or vendor split of the same measures; the table is borough × borough × month only.
- Trips outside the reporting window, non-billable trips, and non-trip sources (shift and fleet-operations measures).
- A schedule or job: the table is built by the existing dbt run, as the other marts are.
