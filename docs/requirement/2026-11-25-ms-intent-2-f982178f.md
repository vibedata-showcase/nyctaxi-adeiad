# Requirement: Borough-to-borough flows for Q1 2024

## Ask

From Leila Haddad, Mobility Planning Lead:

> "The city asked how much of our traffic crosses borough lines. For each month of Q1 2024, I need the trips and the revenue from each borough to each borough, and the share of trips that leave the borough they started in."

A new gold table: one row per month, origin borough and destination borough, carrying billable trips, revenue and the share of that origin borough's trips which end in a different borough. Consumers are the Mobility Planning team and the city's question it answers; it is a published surface, so it owes a contract, tests, a design record and the semantic-model change a new boundary relation brings.

## Requirements

- [ ] `R-01` One row per (month, origin borough, destination borough) for the three months of Q1 2024, over the eight borough labels the zone master carries plus one `unresolved` group, named as `agg_weather_demand_daily` names it, for a trip whose zone key resolves to nothing; a pair-month that had no billable trip is present as zero — accept when the relation has 243 rows (9 groups × 9 groups × 3 months) and the grain `(trip_month, origin_borough, destination_borough)` is unique. The `unresolved` group carries zeros today: no billable in-window trip has a null zone key — source: request; user answer "all 8 labels" and "emit every pair-month from the zone master"; `agg_weather_demand_daily.sql` spine
- [ ] `R-02` `trip_count` counts billable trips picked up in that month in the origin borough whose dropoff zone lies in the destination borough — accept when the sum of `trip_count` over the whole relation is 95,958 and per month 29,985 (2024-01), 30,017 (2024-02) and 35,956 (2024-03), equal to `fct_trip` filtered to `is_billable` inside the reporting window — source: `docs/adr/0003-billable-trip-definition-and-where-it-applies.md`
- [ ] `R-03` `total_revenue` is the sum of `total_amount` over those same trips — accept when its total is 2,629,185.82 and per month 816,057.76 (2024-01), 813,885.68 (2024-02) and 999,242.38 (2024-03) — source: user answer "sum of total_amount over billable trips"; `docs/adr/0013-no-charge-and-dispute-payments-are-not-excluded-by-the-billable-rule.md`
- [ ] `R-04` `cross_borough_share` is the share of the origin borough's billable trips in that month which end in any other borough, and it carries the same value on every row of that origin-month — accept when, for every origin-month, the sum of `trip_count` over rows whose destination differs from the origin equals `cross_borough_share` × `origin_borough_trip_count` within rounding, and Manhattan in 2024-01 reads 6.01% — source: request wording "the share of trips that leave the borough they started in"; user answer "per origin borough and month, repeated on each of its rows"
- [ ] `R-05` The share is published beside its denominator: `origin_borough_trip_count`, the origin borough's total billable trips that month, on every row of the origin-month — accept when dividing the cross-borough trip sum of `R-04` by it reproduces `cross_borough_share` for every origin-month — source: user answer on the share; the transformation kind's Coverage branch (a share published beside the count it divides)
- [ ] `R-06` Unresolved zones and the out-of-state airport are published as their own borough labels, not dropped, remapped or filtered — accept when the relation carries `Unknown`, `N/A` and `EWR` rows and their origin-side trip sums are 360 (Unknown + N/A) and 12 (EWR) over Q1 — source: `docs/adr/0012-unresolved-zones-are-retained-and-flagged.md`; user answer "all 8 labels"
- [ ] `R-07` No trip floor applies at borough-pair grain; a pair-month exists whatever its size, and the table states its population — accept when no row is suppressed for being small and Staten Island's 11 billable January trips appear — source: user answer "no floor"; the departure from `docs/adr/0017-published-flows-have-a-25-trip-floor.md` is recorded as its own ADR before the table ships
- [ ] `R-08` Every billable in-window trip lands in exactly one row, including a trip whose zone key resolves to no borough (attributed to an `unresolved` group rather than dropped) — accept when the relation's `trip_count` sums to the billable in-window count of `fct_trip` and a singular dbt test asserts the equality — source: playbook default "keep every row"; `docs/adr/0015-unmatched-dimension-keys-stay-null.md`
- [ ] `R-09` As a gold boundary relation it ships with its dbt properties and description, generic tests on the grain and measures, a singular conservation test, an ODCS contract under `contracts/`, and a design record under `docs/design/models/` — accept when `dbt build` passes on the model and its tests, `odcs.py check` passes for its contract, and `adr_lint` reports no dangling citation — source: `repo-map.json` `modules.contracts`; `AGENTS.md` "Layers and their roles"

## Business decisions

| Decision | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grain | One row per month × origin borough × destination borough | user answer (spine); request |
| Month attribution | The month of the trip's pickup timestamp, as trips are reported on the day they start | `CONTEXT.md` "Date"; request |
| Borough labels | The eight labels the zone master carries: Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, Bronx, Staten Island, Unknown, N/A, EWR | user answer "all 8 labels"; `core.dim_zone` |
| Trip measure | Billable trips only, inside the reporting window | `docs/adr/0003-billable-trip-definition-and-where-it-applies.md` |
| Revenue measure | Sum of `total_amount` over billable trips, so no-charge and dispute payments are included | user answer; `docs/adr/0013-no-charge-and-dispute-payments-are-not-excluded-by-the-billable-rule.md` |
| Cross-borough share | Cross-borough trips ÷ the origin borough's billable trips, per origin borough and month, repeated on each of its rows, with the denominator published | user answer; request |
| Trip floor | None at borough-pair grain; the ADR 0017 floor is defined for zone pairs and is not applied here | user answer; `docs/adr/0017-published-flows-have-a-25-trip-floor.md` |
| Pair-months with no trips | Emitted as zero rows from a borough spine | user answer |
| Null zone keys | A trip whose zone key is null is attributed to `unresolved`, never dropped | playbook default "keep every row"; `docs/adr/0015-unmatched-dimension-keys-stay-null.md` |
| Edge periods | Not applicable: the loaded window is 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31 and every one of the three months has all its days (31, 29, 31) in the data, so no period is cut short | `core.fct_trip` coverage query; `transformation/dbt_project.yml` vars |
| Coverage of the revenue measure | Not applicable beyond the trip count already published: every trip in the loaded window carries a `total_amount` (97,184 of 97,184), so `trip_count` is itself the count of rows carrying revenue | `core.fct_trip` coverage query |
| Reuse | Built from `fct_trip` with `dim_zone` joined twice, not rolled up from `agg_od_flow_matrix`, whose 25-trip floor drops most borough pairs and would contradict `R-07` | user answer (no floor); `agg_od_flow_matrix.sql` |
| Layers, materialisation, schema, naming | Design questions, recorded for `designing`, not settled here | `capturing-requirements` step 4 |
| Glossary and ADR | A term for a cross-borough trip and the ADR recording the no-floor population are owed at design, with the design record they cite | `domain-modeling`; `adr` "Affected designs" |

## Out of scope

- No change to any published model: `agg_od_flow_matrix`, `fct_trip`, `mart_daily_zone_revenue`, `mart_monthly_service_summary` and every other existing mart keep their code and their population, and `--full-refresh` is not run on any of them.
- No change to ingestion, no new source, no orchestration item or schedule.
- No zone-grain or corridor-level publishing; `agg_od_flow_matrix` remains the zone surface and this table does not replace or alter it.
- No change to the billable rule, the reporting window or the ADR 0017 floor as it applies to the zone matrix.

None blocking. Pending writes, held because this stop ends before design: the ADR recording that a borough flow carries no trip floor (the departure from ADR 0017 named in `R-07`), and the `CONTEXT.md` term for a trip that ends outside its origin borough.
