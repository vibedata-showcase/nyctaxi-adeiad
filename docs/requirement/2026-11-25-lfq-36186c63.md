# Requirement: Borough-to-borough monthly traffic and revenue (LFQ)

## Ask

The city asked how much of the city's taxi traffic crosses borough lines. For each month of Q1 2024 the Mobility Planning Lead needs, as one table: the trips and the revenue from each borough to each borough, and the share of trips that leave the borough they started in. The consumer is the Mobility Planning team, answering a city question about inter-borough traffic.

## Requirements

- [ ] `R-01` One new boundary table gives a row per month of Q1 2024, per origin group and per destination group — accept when the relation has exactly one row for every combination of `3 months × 8 origin groups × 8 destination groups` (192 rows), with `trip_count` 0 and revenue 0 where nothing travelled — source: request; answer "complete grid, zeros included"
- [ ] `R-02` Trips and revenue are reported from each group to each group — accept when `trip_count` is `count(*)` and `total_revenue` is `sum(total_amount)` over the table's population, and `sum(trip_count)` over the whole table equals the billable trips picked up in the reporting window (95,958 at the current build) — source: request; ADR 0003; ADR 0018
- [ ] `R-03` Every month carries the share of trips that left the borough they started in — accept when, per month and origin group, `crossing_share` equals `crossing_trip_count / resolvable_destination_trip_count`, where the numerator is trips whose destination group differs from the origin group and the denominator is trips whose destination group is not one of the two groups that place no zone in a borough — and it is null, never 0 or 1, for an origin group whose resolvable-destination count is 0 — source: request; answer "resolvable destinations only"; ADR 0019
- [ ] `R-04` The population is billable trips inside the reporting window, and no trip floor is applied — accept when the model filters `fct_trip.is_billable` and `in_report_window`, and no pair-month below 25 trips is absent: the 66 observed pair-months under 25 trips (of 122 observed) are all published — source: ADR 0003; ADR 0018
- [ ] `R-05` No trip is dropped, merged or relabelled by the join or the grouping — accept when `sum(trip_count)` over the table equals the billable-trip count in the window, and the eight groups the zone lookup carries each appear as an origin and as a destination — source: ADR 0012; answer "include EWR flagged not-NYC"
- [ ] `R-06` A reader can tell the city boroughs from the out-of-state airport and from the two groups that place no zone in a borough — accept when each row carries whether its origin and destination group is NYC and whether each is an unresolved group, and the descriptions state that a total over all groups is not a city total — source: `CONTEXT.md` "Borough"; ADR 0012; ADR 0018
- [ ] `R-07` The table is documented as a data product: a properties entry with grain, column descriptions and tests on the grain, a design record citing its ADRs, and a data contract where the repository requires one for a new gold relation — accept when `dbt build` passes for the model, its singular tests return no rows for a duplicate-grain check, and the contract gate for the relation passes — source: repository convention (`repo-map.json` `artifact_paths`, `contracts/` description: the gold relations)

## Business decisions

| Decision | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grain of the new mart | One row per month × origin group × destination group, complete grid with zero-trip cells kept | user answer (complete grid); request ("for each month … from each borough to each borough") |
| Edge periods | `not applicable`: the reporting window is 2024-01-01 to 2024-04-01 and Q1 is covered end to end — trips run on 2024-01-01 and 2024-03-31, and every month has all its days (31, 29, 31) — so no month is cut short | recomputed against `core.fct_trip` |
| Population and metric definitions | Billable trips (positive total) inside the reporting window, per ADR 0003; revenue is the sum of `total_amount` over them, no-charge and disputed payments included, per ADR 0013; no trip floor at borough grain, per ADR 0018; the crossing share's denominator is trips whose destination group is a named borough, per ADR 0019 | ADR 0003, ADR 0013, ADR 0018, ADR 0019; user answers |
| Coverage | Revenue is always present on the table's population: `total_amount` is null on 0 of the 95,958 billable trips in the window, so the measure needs no "carries the measure" count of its own; instead the crossing share publishes its own denominator and numerator (`resolvable_destination_trip_count`, `crossing_trip_count`) so the share can be re-derived and its population seen | recomputed against `core.fct_trip`; playbook default (publish the count) |
| Reuse | Build from `fct_trip` with `dim_zone` joined twice, as `agg_od_flow_matrix` does; `agg_od_flow_matrix` cannot be reused because ADR 0017's floor removes most of the cells this table needs, and `agg_trip_zone_daily` carries only the pickup zone, so neither can produce the pairs | inspected model SQL; ADR 0017 |
| Unmatched and null keys | Keep every row: no trip is dropped. Every trip in the window resolves to both zone keys (0 null pickup keys, 0 null dropoff keys of 95,958 billable trips), and each of the eight groups the lookup carries is published as its own group rather than remapped or dropped | recomputed against `core.fct_trip` and `core.dim_zone`; ADR 0012 |
| Boroughs published | The eight groups the zone lookup carries — the five city boroughs, EWR (out of state), and the two groups that place no zone in a borough — with an NYC flag on each row so a city-only reader can filter | user answer "include EWR flagged not-NYC"; ADR 0012; ADR 0018 |
| Extended scope | `not a question`: a `logical-model/` update for the new boundary relation stays in this kind, authored with the model | playbook `transformation` |

## Out of scope

- Any change to `fct_trip`, `dim_zone`, `agg_od_flow_matrix` or any other published model, and any change to ADR 0017's floor for the zone-pair matrix.
- Zone-pair or airport-level detail; this table reports borough groups only.
- A period other than Q1 2024: the reporting window (`report_start`, `report_end`) is unchanged, and the mart reports whatever months that window covers.
- Any change to the ingestion pipelines or to the source data.

## Open questions

None. The three branches that changed a row count or a value — the crossing share's denominator, the trip floor, and the treatment of EWR — were answered by the user in this intent.
