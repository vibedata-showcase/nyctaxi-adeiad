# Requirement: JL

## Ask

Leila Haddad, Mobility Planning Lead, on a question from the city:

> "The city asked how much of our traffic crosses borough lines. For each month of Q1 2024, I need the trips and the revenue from each borough to each borough, and the share of trips that leave the borough they started in."

A new gold relation: a monthly borough-to-borough flow matrix, with each origin borough's cross-borough share. Consumed by Mobility Planning as a city-facing answer, and by anyone asking how much traffic leaves the borough it starts in.

## Requirements

- [ ] `R-01` A monthly borough-to-borough flow relation exists for Q1 2024 with one row per (month, origin borough, destination borough), for every pair carrying at least one billable trip in that month — accept when a grain-uniqueness query over the built relation returns zero duplicated (month, origin, destination) groups and the relation holds exactly 40, 40 and 42 rows for January, February and March 2024, the pairs recomputed independently from `core.fct_trip` for the same months — source: request
- [ ] `R-02` `trip_count` counts the pair's billable trips in the month, so the month's total is the whole billable population — accept when, for each month, `sum(trip_count)` over the built relation equals the billable trip count recomputed independently from `core.fct_trip` for that month (29,985 / 30,017 / 35,956 for January, February and March 2024) — source: request; `docs/adr/0003-billable-trip-definition-and-where-it-applies.md`
- [ ] `R-03` `total_revenue` is `sum(total_amount)` over the pair's billable trips, including no-charge and dispute trips, with the count of trips carrying the measure published beside it — accept when an independent recomputation from `core.fct_trip` over the same population matches the published revenue to the cent for every row of one month, and the published carrying count equals the independently recomputed count of billable trips per pair (every billable trip in Q1 carries a non-null total: 0 nulls in 95,958) — source: request; `docs/adr/0013-no-charge-and-dispute-payments-are-not-excluded-by-the-billable-rule.md`
- [ ] `R-04` Each origin borough's cross-borough share is published on its rows for the month, computed over the trips whose destination borough is resolved, beside its numerator and denominator and the count of trips left out of the denominator — accept when, for every row, `cross_borough_trip_count / origin_resolved_trip_count` recomputes the published share within 1e-9, every row of one (month, origin borough) carries an identical share, `origin_resolved_trip_count + origin_unresolved_trip_count = origin_trip_count` on every row, and one such series is recomputed independently from `core.fct_trip` — source: request; user answer 2026-11-25
- [ ] `R-05` The relation is a boundary relation in `agg`, under an enforced dbt contract with its ODCS contract at `contracts/agg/`, and its columns are documented — accept when `dbt build --select <model>` passes with `contract: enforced`, `odcs.py check` passes, and every column carries a description — source: `repo-map.json` (`layers.agg`, `artifact_paths.data_contract`)

## Out of scope

- Every published model stays untouched: `agg_od_flow_matrix`, `mart_daily_zone_revenue`, `mart_monthly_service_summary`, `agg_weather_demand_daily`, `agg_trip_zone_daypart_dow` and the rest of `core`, `finance` and `ops`. This relation is new; nothing is retyped, removed or rewritten.
- No change to `fct_trip`, `dim_zone`, the ingestion pipelines, `docs/adr/0017` or the 25-trip floor it sets on `agg_od_flow_matrix` itself.
- Trip-level and zone-level flow detail: covered by `agg_od_flow_matrix` and `mart_daily_zone_revenue`.
- Fleet-operations reporting, and anything outside the Q1 2024 window.

## Open questions

1. **Does ADR 0017's 25-trip floor govern this table?** ADR 0017 says "A published origin-destination flow is a zone pair with at least 25 billable trips in the reporting window", and keeps `agg_od_flow_matrix` out of the semantic layer for it. Its stated reason is the 265 × 265 zone cross product of ones and twos, which does not arise at 8 boroughs; but a borough pair is still a published flow. Applying it per pair per month withholds 99 / 55 / 99 trips and moves the cross-borough share from 12.79% / 12.15% / 13.16% to 12.57% / 12.05% / 12.99%. Holds: `R-02`'s reconciliation to the star, and whether the relation can join the semantic layer.
2. **Do unresolved boroughs count in the share?** "Unknown" and "N/A" are one zone each (`dim_zone.is_unresolved_zone`) and carry real volume — 247 Manhattan→Unknown and 221 Unknown→Unknown billable trips in Q1, and in January 3.4% of all Queens-originating trips end in one of them. With them in the denominator Manhattan's January share reads 6.01%; over resolved destinations only it reads 5.55%, and Unknown/N/A as origins read 100% cross-borough by construction. Holds: the published share values in `R-04`.
3. **Which revenue rule?** ADR 0013 leaves it to each metric: billable revenue includes no-charge and dispute trips, recognised revenue does not. Q1 billable revenue is $2,629,185.82, of which 797 Dispute trips carry $20,431.24 and 490 No charge trips $10,879.21 (1.2%); a further 106 billable trips carry $3,551.07 and no payment method at all, which `mart_monthly_service_summary`'s inner join already drops. Including the no-charge and dispute trips matches the other revenue marts; excluding them makes this table disagree with them. Holds: `R-03`'s recomputation.
4. **Zero-activity pairs.** Omit them (about 40 rows a month, consistent with the other `agg` marts) or emit the full 8 × 8 grid per month with zeros (64 rows a month, 192 for the quarter, most of them invented). Holds: `R-01`'s row count.
