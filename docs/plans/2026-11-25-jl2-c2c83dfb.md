# Plan: jl2

**Goal:** Make `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` read `agg.agg_trip_zone_daily` as its only upstream relation, with every one of its 7,961 published rows value-identical to today.

**Requirement:** `docs/requirement/2026-11-25-jl2-c2c83dfb.md`

**Constraints:**
- The report's eleven published columns keep their names, types and order; `trip_date` stays `DATE`.
- The report's grain stays one row per `trip_date` × `pickup_location_id`.
- The agg's existing `trip_count` and `total_revenue` keep their all-trips meaning and values.
- Money is compared at the cent (`round(x, 2)`); DuckDB float aggregation is not reproducible bit for bit.
- No `select *` in a mart; list columns explicitly.
- The sandbox is a renamed copy of the Domain file, so a `dbt` command must not name a database.
