# Plan: RR_Intent — monthly borough-to-borough flow

**Goal:** A boundary aggregate `agg.agg_borough_flow_monthly` with the trips and revenue of every borough pair per Q1 2024 month, and each origin borough's cross-borough share, mapped into the semantic layer and published under contract.

**Requirement:** `docs/requirement/2026-11-25-rr-intent-b2475a59.md`

**Constraints:**
- Layers and naming: a new `agg_` model in `transformation/models/marts/agg/`, logical schema `agg`, materialisation `table` (`dbt-models` B2, B15; `dbt_project.yml`).
- Column names and types fixed by `D-07`: `trip_month DATE`, `origin_borough VARCHAR`, `dest_borough VARCHAR`, `trip_count BIGINT`, `total_revenue DOUBLE`, `cross_borough_trip_count BIGINT`, `cross_borough_share DOUBLE`.
- Population: `fct_trip.is_billable` and `in_report_window(pickup_datetime)`, both zone keys resolved by the as-of joins (ADR 0003, ADR 0015).
- No trip floor (ADR 0018); ADR 0017's 25-trip floor stays on `agg_od_flow_matrix` only.
- Both borough roles keep every `dim_zone.borough_name`, unresolved groups and EWR included; no filter, no remap (ADR 0012, ADR 0018).
- `trip_month` is the pickup month cast to `DATE` (`D-05`).
- `cross_borough_share` is a window ratio over the origin borough-month through `safe_divide`, never an average of per-pair rates (ADR 0016, `D-08`).
- The boundary relation carries `contract: {enforced: true}` and an ODCS contract at `contracts/agg/agg_borough_flow_monthly.odcs.yaml` (R-09, `D-10`).
- The relation joins the `trips` star of `logical-model/nyc_taxi_analytics.ossie.yaml` as one aggregate logical source; no new metric, no ontology mapping (`D-11`, `D-12`).
- No existing published model changes: `agg_od_flow_matrix`, `fct_trip` and `dim_zone` stay as they are.
