# Plan: mich

**Goal:** A gold relation `agg.agg_borough_flow_monthly` giving, for each month of Q1 2024, the billable trips and revenue from each borough to each borough plus the share of each origin borough's trips that cross a borough line, bound into the contracts and the semantic layer.

**Requirement:** `docs/requirement/2026-11-25-mich-8cb8e891.md`

**Constraints:**
- dbt models live under `transformation/`; a new gold model is `agg_`-prefixed in `transformation/models/marts/agg/` and its folder schema is `agg` (`+schema` in `dbt_project.yml`, accepted at design).
- Contract first: the ODCS contract at `contracts/agg/agg_borough_flow_monthly.odcs.yaml` is authored `status: proposed` before the SQL, set `active` once built, and every `data_type`, test, `contract: {enforced: true}` and description on the model comes from `odcs.py generate`, never by hand.
- `access: protected` on a boundary model; explicit column list, no `select *`; cast every expression to its declared type (DuckDB does not coerce to a contract type).
- Billable rule and reporting window applied here in gold (ADR 0003); the window comes from the `report_start` / `report_end` vars through `in_report_window()`, never hard-coded dates.
- Rates are rebuilt through `safe_divide` from summed numerators and denominators (ADR 0016); no floor (ADR 0019); crossing is equality on `dim_zone.borough_name` including unresolved groups and EWR (ADR 0018, ADR 0012).
- One singular test file per assertion, named `assert_<what_must_hold>.sql`; generic `not_null` on keys and measures; unit tests in `transformation/models/marts/agg/_unit_tests.yml`; data and unit tests run as separate commands.
- Nothing published today changes: `agg_od_flow_matrix`, the `finance` marts and the reporting window stay as they are (`R-09`).

- [ ] Author `contracts/agg/agg_borough_flow_monthly.odcs.yaml` with `status: proposed`, `version: 1.0.0`, one property per column of `D-02`–`D-09` (`month_start_date`, `origin_borough`, `destination_borough`, `is_cross_borough`, `billable_trip_count`, `billable_revenue`, `origin_month_trip_count`, `origin_month_crossing_trip_count`, `origin_month_crossing_share`, `month_unplaced_billable_trip_count`), `primaryKey` on the three grain columns, a `dataGranularityDescription` stating the grain, and `nullValues`/`duplicateValues` quality rules on the grain and measures.
- [ ] Author `transformation/models/marts/agg/agg_borough_flow_monthly.sql` in three CTEs: `placed` (the billable in-window trips inner joined to both `dim_zone` roles, grouped to month × origin borough × destination borough with `billable_trip_count` and `billable_revenue`), `origin_month` (the same population summed to month × origin borough, carrying the crossing count as `count(*) filter (where o.borough_name <> dz.borough_name)`), and `unplaced` (billable in-window trips with a null pickup or dropoff zone key, counted per month from `fct_trip` alone, because the inner join is exactly what excludes them). The final select left joins `placed` to `origin_month` and `unplaced`, writes `month_start_date` as `cast(date_trunc('month', f.pickup_date) as date)`, the share through `safe_divide`, `coalesce(u.unplaced_trip_count, 0)` so a month with none reads 0, and casts every output expression to its contract type.
- [ ] Run `python3 .agents/skills/data-contract/scripts/odcs.py generate`, then author `transformation/models/marts/agg/_agg.yml`'s `agg_borough_flow_monthly` entry with `contract: {enforced: true}`, `access: protected`, the generated `data_type` values and column descriptions, and `data_tests: [not_null]` on the three grain columns and the two measures. No `relationships` test on the borough columns: `borough_name` is a level of `dim_zone`, not its key, so the grain test and the unit tests carry that check.
- [ ] Author `transformation/models/marts/agg/_unit_tests.yml` with two cases: a trip within one borough and a trip to another borough (the share and `is_cross_borough` branch), and a pair whose month has no trip in the fixture (no row emitted — `R-06`).
