# Plan: SS · borough flow

**Goal:** Add one boundary table, `agg.agg_borough_flow_monthly`, reporting billable trips, revenue and the cross-borough share from each origin borough to each destination borough for every month of Q1 2024.

**Requirement:** `docs/requirement/2026-11-24-ss-borough-flow-951b6777.md`

**Constraints:**
- Billable only, picked up inside the reporting window (`report_start` 2024-01-01, `report_end` 2024-04-01), per ADR 0003.
- No trip floor and no minimum cell size at this grain, per ADR 0018.
- The share is per origin borough per month, over trips whose destination lies in a borough, published with both counts, per ADR 0019.
- Every trip touching an unresolved zone, or with a null zone key, reports `unresolved` in that role and is neither in-borough nor cross-borough, per ADR 0012 and ADR 0015.
- Borough values are the master data's own, EWR included; no remapping, splitting or dropping.
- New model names follow `agg_<entity>` (`.agents/skills/dbt-models`); the folder's `+schema` in `dbt_project.yml` is untouched.
- No change to `fct_trip`, `dim_zone`, `agg_od_flow_matrix` or any other published model.
- The relation is excluded from the semantic layer, not mapped (design D-12).
