# Plan: Build the daily zone revenue report from the operations figures

**Goal:** Aggregate the billable measures once in `agg_trip_zone_daily` and have `mart_daily_zone_revenue` select them, so one build serves both the operations figures and the finance report, with no number in the report changing.

**Requirement:** `docs/requirement/2026-11-25-tarun-ex2-5f307739.md`

**Constraints:**
- The report publishes exactly its eleven current columns and 7,961 rows; Q1 2024 totals stay 95,958 trips and 2,629,185.82 revenue, and JFK Airport stays 4,120 trips and 337,711.05 revenue (R-04, R-05).
- Existing `agg_trip_zone_daily` columns keep their names, types, population and values: 7,997 rows, `trip_count` and `total_revenue` still all-trips (R-03).
- Seven properties are added to `agg.agg_trip_zone_daily` and none is removed or retyped; its contract moves to 1.1.0 (breaking-contract approval, 2026-11-25).
- The billable rule stays in gold and is applied once, in `agg_trip_zone_daily` (ADR 0003, as amended by the finance design record's `D-02`).
- Money is compared at cent precision, never for bit equality: DuckDB float aggregation is not reproducible bit for bit (`dbt-testing`).
- New column names carry the `billable_` prefix; `trip_count` and `total_revenue` are not renamed.
- Schemas are the recorded defaults: `agg` for `marts/agg`, `finance` for `marts/finance`.
