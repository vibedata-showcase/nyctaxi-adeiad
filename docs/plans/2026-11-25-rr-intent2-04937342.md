# Plan: RR_Intent2 — build the daily zone revenue report from the operations figures

**Goal:** Make `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` a projection of `agg.agg_trip_zone_daily`, so one build produces the daily zone revenue figures, while Finance's published numbers and Finance's four Q1 2024 acceptance figures stay exactly as they are.

**Requirement:** `docs/requirement/2026-11-25-rr-intent2-04937342.md`

**Constraints:**
- The eleven published columns of `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` keep their names, order and types; no column is added, removed or retyped there.
- The fourteen existing columns of `agg.agg_trip_zone_daily` keep their names and their values: `trip_count` and `total_revenue` stay unfiltered.
- The billable rule is applied in exactly one model for this grain, `agg_trip_zone_daily`; the finance mart applies no billable filter ([ADR 0018](../../docs/adr/0018-zone-day-billable-measures-are-computed-once-in-the-aggregate.md)).
- A join to `dim_zone` for zone attributes carries the half-open validity interval on the trip date, never the natural key alone ([ADR 0009](../../docs/adr/0009-type-2-only-where-the-source-versions-history.md)).
- Money compares at the cent (`round(x, 2)`); a `double` sum is never compared bit for bit.
- Both dbt folders keep their current schema: `agg` and `finance`.
- The reporting window is `report_start`/`report_end` in `dbt_project.yml`; it is not changed, and no model re-derives it.
- Contracts are authored first and their dbt keys written by `odcs.py generate`, never by hand.
- `dbt deps` runs in `transformation/` before the session's first dbt command, because each session starts from a fresh clone with no `dbt_packages/`.
