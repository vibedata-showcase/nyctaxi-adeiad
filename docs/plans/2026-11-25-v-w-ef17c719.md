# Plan: Borough-to-borough trip and revenue flows for Q1 2024

**Goal:** A monthly borough-to-borough table for Q1 2024 with billable trips, revenue and each origin borough's crossing share, published over the whole billable population.

**Requirement:** [`docs/requirement/2026-11-25-v-w-ef17c719.md`](../requirement/2026-11-25-v-w-ef17c719.md)

**Constraints:**

- Grain: one row per (trip month, origin borough group, destination borough group) over the full grid of nine borough groups; no minimum-traffic floor.
- Billable trips only, inside the reporting window 2024-01-01 to 2024-04-01 exclusive ([ADR 0003](../adr/0003-billable-trip-definition-and-where-it-applies.md)).
- Unresolved zones (`Unknown`, `N/A`) stay as their own groups, and a trip with no matching zone groups as `unresolved`; never dropped or remapped ([ADR 0012](../adr/0012-unresolved-zones-are-retained-and-flagged.md), [ADR 0015](../adr/0015-unmatched-dimension-keys-stay-null.md)).
- The borough surface carries no traffic floor; the 25-trip floor stays scoped to the zone-pair matrix ([ADR 0018](../adr/0018-a-borough-grain-flow-carries-no-traffic-floor.md)).
- The crossing share is over resolved destinations, with its denominator and its excluded unresolved count beside it; null for an unresolved origin.
- Zone attribution is as of the trip's pickup date through the surrogate key ([ADR 0009](../adr/0009-type-2-only-where-the-source-versions-history.md)).
- Money is compared at the cent; the model lives in the `agg` folder, whose schema is `agg`.
