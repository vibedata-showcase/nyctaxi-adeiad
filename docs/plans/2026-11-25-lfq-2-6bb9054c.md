# Plan: LFQ 2

**Goal:** Build `finance.mart_daily_zone_revenue` from `agg.agg_trip_zone_daily` so the billable rule and the zone aggregation are computed once, with no published number changing.

**Requirement:** `docs/requirement/2026-11-25-lfq-2-6bb9054c.md`

**Constraints:**
- The report's 11 columns keep their names, types and grain: one row per pickup zone and trip date.
- The report publishes a row only where the zone-day has at least one billable trip.
- Q1 2024 must show 95,958 trips and 2,629,185.82 revenue in total, and 4,120 trips and 337,711.05 revenue for JFK Airport.
- `agg_trip_zone_daily`'s existing `trip_count` and `total_revenue` keep their population-wide meaning and values.
- `agg_trip_zone_daily` keeps every in-window zone-day: it adds columns, never rows.
- Float measures are compared at the cent and counts exactly; never bit-for-bit, never by hashing.
- Both dbt folders keep their current schema: `agg` and `finance`.
- The billable rule is applied in exactly one place on this path, inside the reporting window.
